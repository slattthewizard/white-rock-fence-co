If you search for the cost to replace fence in Dallas, you will find two published numbers that look like they cannot both be true. One DFW firm advertises installed cedar privacy fence at $18 to $28 per linear foot. Another advertises $30 to $60 per linear foot for a standard six-foot wood privacy fence.

Both are real advertised ranges, and the gap between them is the honest answer. Price depends on the wood, the height, the style, the access to your yard, what happens to the old fence and who is writing the quote. This guide shows what firms publish, what moves the number, and how to compare bids without getting burned.

## Why the cost to replace a fence swings so far between quotes

Six things move the number more than anything else. Know them before you compare bids.

**Wood.** One DFW firm says cedar typically costs 20 to 35 percent more in materials than pressure-treated pine. That is a materials figure, not a finished-fence figure, so do not apply it to a whole quote.

**Style.** One DFW firm says board-on-board and shadow box styles typically add $2 to $5 per linear foot over a basic dog-ear fence. Another says board-on-board adds about 30 percent to a regular wood fence. Both are single-firm claims.

**Height.** National totals from Fixr for 150 feet run $2,250 to $6,000 at 6 ft, $3,150 to $9,750 at 7 ft and $4,500 to $12,750 at 8 ft. Those are national, not Dallas. If you want 8 ft, make every bidder price 8 ft; do not let a 6-ft number stand in for it.

**Gates.** A walk gate adds $150 to $400 and a double drive gate $400 to $900, according to one DFW firm. A different page from the same firm says $200 to $450 and $500 to $1,000. Automatic gates are a separate budget: Fixr puts national electric gate installs at $2,500 to $5,500, with a national average of $4,000. If your existing gate already has an opener, read [our gate opener repair guide](/blog/gate-opener-repair/) before you assume it needs replacing.

**Your site.** Slope, trees, a tight alley, a shared line and old concrete footings all change labor. Much of the Dallas area sits on expansive clay that swells and shrinks with moisture, so ask what each quote assumes about posts and how they are set. Contractors disagree on post depth and no neutral source gives one, so judge the answer, not a number.

**Old fence removal and haul-off.** No Dallas price for this was sourced. Ask every bidder whether tear-out and disposal are in the number or extra.

## Three examples of what a replacement looks like in dollars

These are anonymised illustrations, not customer stories. The prices are the advertised ranges above applied to a typical situation.

**A 1972 ranch house in Lake Highlands with a leaning alley fence.** Roughly 150 feet of six-foot cedar, posts soft at ground level. One Dallas firm advertises straightening a leaning 10 to 30 ft section at $300 to $800, which only works when the posts are still sound. Two sections would come to $600 to $1,600 by multiplication, against $3,000 to $4,500 for a full cedar replacement from one DFW firm. The cheaper repair is the better deal only if the fence behind it has years left; see [how long a wood fence lasts](/blog/how-long-does-a-wood-fence-last/). Because the fence runs along an alley, ask the contractor about the City's visibility rules: Dallas code limits items between 2-1/2 ft and 8 ft in height within a 20-ft triangle at a driveway or alley edge and the street curb line.

**A Plano family whose fence blew down in a May storm, with a dog in the yard.** National Weather Service Fort Worth defines a severe thunderstorm as one with damaging winds of 58 mph or more, and North Texas gets them. One Dallas firm advertises a full panel rebuild after wind damage (20 to 50 ft) at $1,200 to $3,500, and another DFW firm lists storm damage repair at $300 to $1,200. The dog is the real deadline, so ask whether the contractor can secure the gap while the permanent work waits. Plano's own handout says a permit is not required for repairs to existing fences not exceeding 25 percent of the fence area in a 12-month period, so a partial rebuild and a full replacement can land on different sides of that line. Confirm with the City of Plano.

**A Richardson owner comparing pine and cedar for an 8-ft board-on-board fence.** This is the case where published Dallas numbers run out. The pine and cedar totals above are for 6-ft fence, board-on-board adds to them, and 8 ft adds more again. The owner's useful move is to ask two or three contractors to price the same height, same style, same posts, once in pine and once in cedar. ## Repair or replace: the numbers that decide it

A repair quote and a replacement quote should sit side by side before you decide. Advertised repair figures from DFW firms:

- Reset a leaning post: $75 to $200 (one DFW firm).
- Pull a post and set it with a new concrete footing: $150 to $400 (one Dallas firm).
- Replace 5 to 10 boards: $200 to $500 (one DFW firm).
- Rebuild a full panel run after wind, 20 to 50 ft: $1,200 to $3,500 (one Dallas firm).

Small jobs also carry a minimum. No Dallas service-call fee was found, but one DFW firm says its repairs start at $450, and a national guide notes that repair companies often have minimum call-out charges. A single loose picket can cost more than the picket.

## Permits, HOAs and the shared back fence

Replacement is not always a swap. Before you sign, check four things.

**The City.** Dallas's fence permit checklist (form 26-001, dated 6/10/2026) says a permit is required for fences exceeding 4 ft in a front yard or 6 ft in all other locations, with a maximum height of 9 ft. The City's own guide says you do not need a permit for fences that meet those limits, except in areas such as historic, conservation and planned development districts. The same guide says applications require you to add a general contractor. Plywood, sheet metal, corrugated metal and fiberglass panels are on the City's prohibited materials list. No Dallas fence permit fee was found, so ask. Read the [City of Dallas fence permit checklist](https://www.dallascityhall.com/departments/sustainabledevelopment/DCH%20documents/FENCE_PERMIT_CHECKILST_26001.pdf) and confirm with the City, because rules differ by suburb: Richardson wants a permit at 2-1/2 ft or taller, Irving for any new or replacement fence, and Carrollton for a new fence over 20 ft in length.

**The contractor.** Texas does not license fence contractors at the state level, and some cities register them, including Dallas, Richardson and Irving. Ask for city registration and proof of insurance, and get both in writing. Never assume a contractor has either because the quote looks professional.

**Digging.** Texas law requires you or your contractor to contact 811 two business days before you dig, even in your own backyard. If you hire a contractor, that call is the contractor's responsibility, according to [Texas811](https://texas811.org/homeowner/). Ask who is making it.

**Your HOA.** Under Texas Property Code Sec. 202.023, an association cannot adopt or enforce a covenant that prevents a perimeter fence, but it can still regulate the type of fencing. Get written approval of the style and height before ordering.

A shared back fence adds one more question. Texas Law Help, a legal aid source, says that where both neighbours use a fence straddling the boundary, both are generally treated as owning it and sharing the cost of repair and replacement. A fence wholly on one lot belongs to that owner. This is general information, not legal advice, and a survey and a conversation with your neighbour come first.

## How to get fence replacement quotes you can compare

Two bids are useless if they price different fences. Ask each contractor to put the following in writing:

- Linear feet, height and style (dog-ear, board-on-board, shadow box).
- Wood species and post material, and how posts are set.
- Number and type of gates, and whether any are automatic.
- Tear-out and haul-off of the old fence, in or out.
- Who applies for the permit, who calls 811, and who handles HOA paperwork.
- City registration and proof of insurance.
- The payment schedule and what happens if the work changes.

Then compare the totals line by line. A bid that is thousands lower usually dropped something from this list. If you are planning a full run, [fence installation in Dallas](/fence-installation-dallas/) covers what to expect from the process.
